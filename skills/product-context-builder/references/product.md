# PRODUCT.md guide

`PRODUCT.md` captures the judgment that cannot be recovered from tokens or component code.

Establish with the user:

- product surface: product UI, marketing site, internal tool, or mixed
- primary and secondary users, including meaningful differences between them
- purpose and the shortest successful loop
- what the product deliberately does not become
- three to five personality qualities, each explained through behavior
- references and the specific quality borrowed from each
- anti-references and the concrete patterns to avoid
- product principles that resolve likely design decisions
- accessibility and domain requirements

Use concrete product language. A reference such as “Linear” is incomplete until the desired quality is named. Avoid broad adjectives that could describe any SaaS product.
