# Foundations and attribution

The interview and brain structure are an original synthesis, not an official NN/g or Impeccable framework. User-selected criteria and priorities are personal defaults, not validated measurements of design quality.

- [NN/g Design Critiques](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/design-critiques/): scope and agreed goals; feedback as a conversation about work.
- [NN/g Cognitive Walkthroughs](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/cognitive-walkthroughs/): task-based examination of learnability. Adapted journey questions do not replace actual user research.
- [NN/g Heuristic Evaluation](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-to-conduct-a-heuristic-evaluation/) and [ten heuristics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/): structured usability criteria. The four ranking options are a simplified selection, not a complete heuristic evaluation.
- [NN/g Derailed Critiques](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/derailed-design-critiques/): distinguish goals and evidence from subjective or hypothetical claims.
- [NN/g Closing the Loop](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/after-design-critique/): connect feedback to decisions, reasons, and next steps.
- [Liz Lerman's Critical Response Process](https://lizlerman.com/critical-response-process/): neutral questions and maker agency inspire exploratory feedback. Our interview does not reproduce the full process.
- [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable): context-gathering interview, saved project documents, and later reuse inform our setup pattern. We build personal practice rather than product context; our update workflow is separate.
- [Agent Skills specification](https://agentskills.io/specification): portable folder format, required SKILL.md, and optional references.
- [OpenAI skill documentation](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/build-skills), [Claude skills](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12512180-use-skills-in-claude), [Cursor skills](https://cursor.com/docs/skills), [Gemini CLI skills](https://geminicli.com/docs/cli/skills/): consult for current host installation and capabilities. Compatibility of the format does not guarantee identical runtime behavior.

No external skill, source subscription, browser, or reference integration is required to conduct this interview.
